Let's get your sign connected

WiFi Network
Enter the name of the WiFi network your sign will connect to.

On your iPhone, go to Settings → Wi-Fi. The network you're connected to will be shown at the top with a checkmark. Tap the (i) icon next to it to see the full network name.

WiFi Password
Enter the password for your WiFi network.

On your iPhone, go to Settings → Wi-Fi, tap the (i) icon next to your network, then tap Password. Authenticate with Face ID or your passcode to reveal it.
